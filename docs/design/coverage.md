**Reference:** the topic sequence of the Masters Spanish Academy level courses
(A1: 27 lessons, A2: 31, B1: 36, B2.1: 18, B2.2: 49 — about 162 lessons in
all). Their *order* is a tested CEFR syllabus; their lessons, vocabulary and
explanations are theirs, and none of it is copied. This is a checklist of
topics and nothing else.

**Ours:** the units in `content/es/`, each ten words.

## What the comparison says

**One structural difference, and it is big.** The reference interleaves
*everyday topics* with the grammar, early: weather, colours, clothes, the
house, food, family, days, shopping, health, the neighbourhood. Our path is
grammar-first-and-late: we teach the subjunctive at B2, where the reference
teaches it at B1.

**Two holes.**

1. **The vocabulary hole, at the level where learners need words.** A1 and A2
   cover the grammar but skip the everyday nouns a beginner actually uses. A
   learner who finishes our A1 can conjugate *-ar* verbs and cannot say what
   the weather is or what colour something is.
2. **The grammar hole, in the middle and the top.** The future, the conditionals,
   the subjunctive, reported speech, the pluperfect, relative clauses, the
   imperfect, the commands, the imperfect and perfect tenses, the passive
   voice — most of what makes a learner intermediate and advanced is absent or
   parked in B2 where it is too late.

## What we would change

**1. Add a topic strand to A1 and A2.** Nine new units, each ten words:
weather · colours and clothes · the house · the family · the days of the week ·
shopping · food and ordering · the body and health · the neighbourhood. That
takes A1 to ten units and A2 to twelve.

**2. Add the missing grammar, and move it earlier.**

| Grammar | Currently | Should be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hay, gustar | words only | A1 |
| por vs para | missing | A1 |
| Superlatives | B1/24 | A1 |

| Pretérito imperfecto | missing | A2 |

**3. Leave B2 for what is genuinely advanced:** the passive voice, the past
subjunctive, the participle, the compound infinitive, nominalisation,
connectors, adjective position, advanced ser and estar, Newsweek verbs.

The result is a path of about fifty units instead of thirty-six, which is the
honest size of A1 to B2 for someone starting from nothing.
